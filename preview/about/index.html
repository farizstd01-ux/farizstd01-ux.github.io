About Shield Tech

Shield Tech Steel Manufacturing LLC builds EMC anechoic chambers, antenna measurement chambers, Faraday cages and shielded rooms in Abu Dhabi.

Request a quoteAll solutions

Two decades of chamber experience, built for the UAE market

We bring more than twenty years of experience in EMC anechoic chambers, antenna chambers and Wi-Fi chambers to the region. Our work also covers cyber security and forensic Faraday cages, removable shielded containers and a wide range of other shielding technologies.

Working closely with Kemar EMC, we plan every project around the specific needs of the client, drawing on years of industry experience. Where a standard product does not fit, we offer custom design solutions.

How we work

The same five steps apply from a compact shielded room to a 10 m chamber.

  1. Brief and survey

    We review your test standard, equipment, frequency range, footprint and site conditions.

  2. Design and engineering

    Layout, shielding targets, absorbers, doors and services are drawn up for your approval.

  3. Manufacture

    Steel structure and shielding modules are fabricated at our KEZAD facility in Abu Dhabi.

  4. Installation

    Our installation teams and delivery partner assemble, align and connect the facility on your site.

  5. Test and handover

    Shielding performance is verified and the facility is handed over with documentation.
